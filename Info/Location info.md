According to Ministry of Food and Agriculture (MOFA)

**Geographic Pilot Area:** Ejura Sekyedumasi

**Dominant Crops:** maize, yams, cassava, cowpea groundnuts, plantain, guinea corn and rice.Vegetables such as garden eggs, okro, tomatoes and pepper

Source: https://mofa.gov.gh/site/directorates/55-district-directorates/district-ashanti/164-ejura-sekyeredumase

**Challenges at the Area:** 

Erratic Rainfall and Prolonged Dry Spells

Severe Pest Invasions (Fall Armyworm)

Destructive Windstorms

Bushfires and Deforestation

**Existing farming practices:**

Rain-Fed Reliance

The Two-Season System

The Maize Monoculture
*"Currently, 88% of Ejura-Sekyedumase relies on rain-fed, continuous maize monoculture heavily dependent on synthetic fertilizers. By using NASA SMAP data to predict dry spells, our tool guides them to rotate in drought-tolerant cowpeas—disrupting the fall armyworm cycle naturally and reducing their expensive fertilizer dependency."*

**Crops to use in rotation scenarios:**

Cereals (The Baseline Cash Crops): maize, rice

Legumes (Nitrogen Fixers): Cowpea, Groundnut

Root and Tuber Crops (The Soil Breakers): Yam, Cassava

Cover Crops \& Green Manures (The Biological Armor): Peas.

**Farmer Priorities:**

Market Suitability \& Income (The #1 Priority)

Input Cost Reduction (Fertilizer \& Pesticide)

Pest Cycle Interruption (The Fall Armyworm)

Water Use \& Drought Resilience

Labor and Equipment Match
