Major Season Onset
March 22 – April 8

Triggered by the northward migration of the ITCZ. Sowing must be held until GPM records 3-day rainfall accumulation ≥ 20 mm to prevent seedling mortality.

August Mid-Season Hiatus
July 28 – August 20

Bimodal rainfall depression. Topsoil dries to 0.15 $m^3/m^3$, but SMAP subsoil root-zone (0–100 cm) maintains 0.22 $m^3/m^3$, sustaining deep-rooted cassava.

Minor Season Cessation
November 12 – 25

Abrupt termination followed by dry northeasterly Harmattan winds. Minor season crops must possess short maturity cycles (≤ 65 days like cowpea).

12-Month Field Action Calendar & Operations (Ejura, Ghana)

Month NASA Weather Condition Field Management Action Resource / Input Guide
Jan – Feb Harmattan Dry, High VPD, High Fire Risk Maintain crop residue mulch on field surface. Construct firebreaks. Zero pre-season burning. Retain previous stover on soil (shields against erosion MFI > 140).
March Major Season Onset (March 22–31) Plant Drought-Tolerant Maize (Omankwa) after GPM 3-day rainfall ≥ 20 mm. Seed rate 20 kg/ha; certified drought-tolerant seed.
April Rapid vegetative growth, high solar radiation Relay intercrop improved Cassava cuttings (Bankyehemaa) between maize rows at 4 weeks. Cassava stem cuttings (10,000 cuttings/ha). Basal micro-dose fertilizer.
May – Jun Peak Major Season Rains (NDVI ~0.74) Monitor Fall Armyworm; scout cassava canopy vigor with Landsat NDVI. Biopesticide / Neem oil if FAW threshold > 15% leaf damage.
July – Aug Mid-Season Hiatus / Dry Spell Vulnerability Harvest maize grain at physiological maturity; leave standing stalks. Cassava roots tap subsoil (0–100 cm). Solar dry maize to ≤ 13% moisture using NASA POWER dry-down window.
Sep (Early) Minor Season Re-onset (Sep 5–12) Direct-drill short-duration Cowpea (Songotra) alongside standing cassava. Inoculate with Bradyrhizobium rhizobia biofertilizer (+15 kg N/ha credit).
October Peak Minor Season Rains (NDVI ~0.66) Cowpea pod formation; full living canopy prevents soil erosion. Zero synthetic N applied; natural atmospheric N-fixation in progress.
Nov – Dec Harmattan Cessation (Nov 15) Harvest cowpea pods. Cassava continues growing throughout the dry season with deep root tap. Market cowpea pulses at peak minor season market price (GHS 12/kg).

1. Growing Degree Days (GDD) Formulation

GDD = max(0, ((T_max + T_min) / 2) - T_base)

Calculated with Base 10°C for Maize and Cowpea (upper cutoff 30°C), and Base 15°C for Cassava and Yam to simulate thermal heat units.

2. Soil Moisture Readily Available Water (RAW)

RAW = p * (theta_FC - theta_WP) * Z_r

For Chromic Luvisols in Ejura: field capacity $\theta_{\text{FC}} = 0.28\text{ m}^3/\text{m}^3$, wilting point $\theta_{\text{WP}} = 0.12\text{ m}^3/\text{m}^3$, depletion fraction $p = 0.55$.

3. Rainfall Erosivity Index (MFI)

MFI = sum((p_i)^2) / P_annual

Ejura decadal MFI = 143.2 (High Erosivity Class > 120), mathematically demonstrating why bare soil monoculture causes catastrophic topsoil loss.

4. Standardized Drought Anomaly Z-Score

Z = (theta_t - mu_doy) / sigma_doy

Evaluated against 10-year day-of-year climatology; $Z \le -1.0$ triggers agricultural drought stress status and in-season contingency mulching.
